If you searched for **OKX bank transfer**, you probably want to know three practical things: whether you can connect a bank account, how much a transfer costs, and when your money becomes available.

For U.S. users, OKX supports bank-account funding through **ACH** in eligible states. Verified users can link a bank account through Plaid, deposit USD or USDG, and use the balance to buy crypto. The main detail to understand is that funds may be available for trading before they are available for withdrawal. That distinction is easy to miss and can cause confusion when a balance appears in the account but cannot yet be sent back to a bank.

This guide explains how the process works, what fees to expect, how to buy crypto after depositing money, and what to check if your bank transfer is delayed or rejected.

## How to deposit USD through OKX bank transfer

After the deposit is initiated, OKX says the funds can generally be used for trading immediately. You may see the USD balance in your account before the ACH transaction has fully cleared through the banking system.

### On the OKX app

4. Choose **Bank transfer (ACH)**.

The app also provides access to deposit history and information about when a deposit becomes available for withdrawal. This is useful because the amount available for trading may be different from the amount currently available to withdraw.

## How long does an ACH deposit take?

There are two different timelines to keep in mind:

- **Trading availability:** OKX says ACH deposits can be available for trading immediately after the deposit is completed in the platform.
- **Bank settlement:** The ACH transaction may take several business days to fully clear through the banking system.

OKX states that ACH deposits typically take **three to five business days** to complete after submission. The exact timing can vary based on banking batches, weekends, holidays, account checks, and the financial institution involved.

This is why an OKX balance can show funds that are usable for trading while the same funds remain temporarily locked for withdrawal. The platform applies a security hold while the bank transfer is being verified and cleared. In some cases, OKX says an additional hold of up to **15 calendar days** may apply for risk-management reasons.

That restriction matters if your plan is to deposit dollars, buy crypto, and immediately send the crypto to another wallet. You may be able to trade right away, but withdrawals can remain restricted until the payment finishes clearing.

> A balance marked as available for trading does not automatically mean it is available for withdrawal.

## Why can I trade but not withdraw?

This is the most common point of confusion with OKX bank transfers.

When an ACH deposit arrives in the platform, OKX may make it available for trading immediately. However, the bank transfer itself may still be processing. Until the payment clears, OKX can restrict withdrawals to reduce fraud and chargeback risk.

Common reasons for a withdrawal restriction include:

- The ACH deposit has not fully settled.
- The account is subject to a temporary security hold.
- An additional risk-management hold has been applied.
- The withdrawal method is not available in your state.
- The linked bank account has not passed ownership verification.
- The name on the bank account does not match your OKX account.
- The funds were deposited recently and are still being reviewed.

You can usually check the estimated withdrawal availability by opening the specific deposit transaction and viewing the information shown beside the withdrawal-availability status.

## What to do if your bank account will not link

Plaid supports many major U.S. banks and credit unions, but a connection can still fail. OKX suggests several practical steps:

1. Try linking the account again later.
2. Confirm that the bank account is in your own name.
3. Check that your personal information matches your OKX profile.
4. Remove the existing connection and relink it through Plaid.
5. Try another eligible bank account.
6. Use a domestic wire-transfer option if ACH linking continues to fail.
7. Contact OKX support with the bank name and error message.

A failed Plaid connection does not necessarily mean that your bank is unsupported. It can result from a temporary connection issue, an interrupted authorization, a security check, or account information that does not match.

## ACH versus domestic wire transfer

ACH is usually more convenient for ordinary deposits and smaller purchases because the bank account can be linked electronically through Plaid. It is useful for users who want to fund an account from a standard U.S. checking or savings account.

A domestic wire transfer may be worth considering when:

- ACH is not available for your bank.
- Your Plaid connection repeatedly fails.
- You need a different transfer method for a larger deposit.
- You are comfortable following bank-wire instructions manually.

The available methods, limits, and settlement timelines depend on your verified account and location. A wire transfer may also involve fees charged by your bank, intermediary institutions, or the receiving service. The exact amount should be confirmed with both banks before sending money.
